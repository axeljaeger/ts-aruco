ts-aruco

ArUco marker detection for the web, ported from Juan Mellado's js-aruco to strict TypeScript. Greyscale, blur and adaptive threshold run on the GPU with WebGL2 or WebGPU, bit-exact with the original.

1

Put a marker on your phone

Scan the code with your phone. It opens a page that shows the marker full screen and keeps the display on. Turn the brightness up.

2

Fly a plane

Open a demo on your computer, allow the webcam and hold the phone up to it. A little plane sits on the marker. Move the phone around and it leaves a smoke trail behind it.

three.js

WebGPURenderer, or its WebGL2 backend without WebGPU. Preprocessing shares the renderer's GPU device.

WebGPU WebGL2

Babylon.js

WebGPU engine, or the WebGL2 engine without WebGPU. Preprocessing shares the engine's GPU device.

WebGPU WebGL2

Babylon Lite

The new, WebGPU-only Babylon. Preprocessing uses its own WebGPU device.

WebGPU

The demos use WebGPU wherever the browser offers it, then WebGL2, then the CPU. The selector at the top of a demo picks the preprocessing by hand, e.g. three.js with CPU preprocessing; ?renderer=webgl2 forces the WebGL2 renderer.

3

The original demos

Juan Mellado's js-aruco samples, unchanged, running the original JavaScript.

  • Marker detector: webcam with marker outlines and ids.
  • Debug view: greyscale, threshold, contours, polygons and candidates.
  • 3D pose: POSIT pose estimation with an old three.js.

The Flash webcam sample of the original is left out: no browser runs Flash any more.
